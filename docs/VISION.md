# Vision

## App Name
**Mermaid Spells**

## Tagline
*Secret underwater letter magic*

## The Problem

Montessori children ages 4-5 are in the sensitive period for writing and reading. They learn cursive first (before print), which aligns with their natural hand rhythm. But:

- **Practice requires repetition**, and repetition can feel boring
- **Children with ADHD** lose focus quickly without frequent dopamine hits
- **Children with performance anxiety** know things privately but freeze when observed
- **Existing letter apps** treat learning as testing — scores, timers, pass/fail

There's no app that combines Montessori cursive-first pedagogy with ADHD-friendly design in a private, anxiety-safe environment.

## The Solution

An underwater world where a friendly mermaid companion helps children practice cursive letters through:

1. **Letter recognition** — match cursive letters to pictures (the restaurant game)
2. **Letter tracing** — draw cursive letters with guided stroke order
3. **Spaced repetition** — letters appear more/less often based on mastery (invisible to child)
4. **Multi-sensory feedback** — visual + audio + haptic reinforcement

All wrapped in a mermaid-themed narrative: each letter is a "spell" the mermaid learns.

## Why It Works (The Restaurant Test)

The original prototype worked because it was:

| Factor | Why It Matters |
|--------|---------------|
| **Private** | No teacher/parent watching. Child explores freely. |
| **Themed** | Mermaids, not "school." Emotional engagement. |
| **Low-stakes** | Guessing game, not test. Wrong answers are fine. |
| **Immediate feedback** | Sounds play, mermaid dances. Instant dopamine. |
| **Short sessions** | Restaurant wait time = natural 5-7 minute session. |
| **Autonomy** | Child taps when ready. No forced pacing. |

## Target Users

### Primary: Montessori Students (4-5)
- Learning cursive as first writing system
- In sensitive period for writing (peaks 3.5-4.5) and reading (peaks 4-5)
- Familiar with sandpaper letters, moveable alphabet, and tracing

### Secondary: Children with ADHD
- Need short bursts (5-7 minutes) not marathons
- Respond to social rewards (smiling character) over material rewards
- Thrive with clean, uncluttered interfaces
- Benefit from flexible pacing (no deadlines)

### Tertiary: Children with Performance Anxiety
- Know things privately but freeze when observed
- Need private practice spaces without evaluation pressure
- Benefit from narrative framing ("helping the mermaid") not testing framing
- Need autonomy over when to demonstrate knowledge

## North Star

If a child picks up the app on their own, plays for 5-7 minutes, puts it down smiling, and without realizing it has practiced the same letter 4 times with increasing accuracy — we've succeeded.

## What This Is NOT

- Not a game with levels, lives, or leaderboards
- Not a school tool with teacher dashboards or grade tracking
- Not a reward system with in-app purchases or unlockable content behind paywalls
- Not a replacement for Montessori materials (sandpaper letters, moveable alphabet)
- It's a **supplement** — private practice that makes classroom learning stick
